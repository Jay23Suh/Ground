# Privacy Policy — Ground

**Last updated: June 2026**

## Who we are

Ground is a personal journaling and mindfulness app for macOS. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and your rights over it.

Contact: jayyy.suh@gmail.com

---

## What we collect

### Account data
- **Email address and name** — required to create and access your account, stored securely via Supabase Auth.

### Journal content
- **Journal entries and notes** — your written responses to prompts and personal notes. These are stored on our servers and are only accessible by you.
- **Date and place metadata** — optional dates you attach to memories, and place names extracted automatically from your text using Apple's on-device Natural Language framework. This extraction happens entirely on your device before any data is sent to our servers.

### Social features (optional)
- **Username / @handle** — a public identifier you choose so friends can find you.
- **Friendship connections** — if you use the friend feature, we store mutual connections.
- **Shared memories** — journal perspectives you choose to share with specific friends.

### Usage data
- **Last active timestamps** — used to time check-in reminders. Not used for analytics.

---

## What we do NOT collect

- We do not use advertising or tracking SDKs.
- We do not sell your data.
- We do not share your data with third parties except as described below.

---

## Third-party services

| Service | Purpose | Data sent |
|---|---|---|
| **Supabase** (supabase.com) | Database and authentication | All user content listed above |
| **zenquotes.io** | Daily inspirational quote | Your IP address only — no account data |

Place name extraction from journal text uses **Apple's Natural Language framework**, which runs entirely on-device. The extracted place name (e.g. "home", "café") may be stored on our servers as metadata attached to your note.

---

## How we protect your data

- All data in transit is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.
- Supabase enforces Row Level Security (RLS) — your data is only accessible by your account.
- We do not store passwords in plain text; authentication is handled by Supabase Auth using industry-standard hashing.

---

## Your rights

You have the right to:

- **Access** your data — all your content is visible within the app.
- **Delete** your data — use Settings → Delete Account to permanently erase all your journal entries, notes, memories, and account. This is irreversible.
- **Export** your data — contact us and we will provide a copy of your data within 30 days.
- **Correct** your data — edit any entry or note at any time within the app.

For GDPR and CCPA requests, contact us at jayyy.suh@gmail.com.

---

## Data retention

If you delete your account, all your data is deleted immediately and cannot be recovered. If you do not delete your account, we retain your data for as long as your account is active.

---

## Children

Ground is not directed at children under 17. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 17.

---

## Changes to this policy

We will notify you of material changes via the app. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
